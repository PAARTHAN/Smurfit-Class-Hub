Who can do what

EveryonePost deadlines, exams, events, jobs and updates · plan hangouts and polls · chat · play games · edit or delete their own posts and messages
Class AmbassadorsEverything above, plus edit or delete anyone's post or message if the info is wrong · pin class updates · manage the invite list

Every edit shows who made it, so the class can always see when an ambassador changed something.

Your data

  • What's storedYour name and UCD email, plus whatever you post: deadlines, updates, plans, poll votes, chat messages and game moves.
  • Who can see itOnly classmates signed in with a UCD account. Nothing is public and nothing is sold or shared.
  • Where it livesIn the class's own database, in an EU data centre.
  • Your rightsDelete any of your posts or messages at any time. Leaving the hub removes your profile, and can remove everything you've posted too.
